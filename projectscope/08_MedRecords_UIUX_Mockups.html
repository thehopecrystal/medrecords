6. Record Category Reference

The 12 record categories defined in the PRD, each rendered as a tab in the patient and provider apps.

🩺 General Medical History
FHIR: Patient, Condition, FamilyMemberHistory. Demographics, family history, chronic conditions, problem list.
🏥 Encounters & Visits
FHIR: Encounter, ClinicalImpression, CarePlan. Outpatient, inpatient, and emergency visits with full notes.
🔪 Surgeries & Procedures
FHIR: Procedure. Surgical history with operative notes, complications, surgeon attribution.
🛏️ Hospitalizations
FHIR: Encounter (IMP), EpisodeOfCare. Admissions with discharge summaries and length of stay.
℞ Prescriptions
FHIR: MedicationRequest. Active and historical Rx with dosing, prescriber, indication.
💊 Pharmacy Dispenses
FHIR: MedicationDispense. What was actually dispensed, when, by whom, in what quantity.
🦷 Dental Records
FHIR: Procedure, Observation, DocumentReference. Tooth chart, restorations, X-rays, cleanings.
👁 Eye Care
FHIR: Observation, VisionPrescription. Refractions, IOP, fundus, optical prescriptions.
🧪 Lab Results
FHIR: DiagnosticReport, Observation, Specimen. LOINC-coded results with reference ranges.
💉 Vaccinations
FHIR: Immunization. Childhood and adult vaccines, due dates, batch numbers.
⚠ Allergies & Conditions
FHIR: AllergyIntolerance, Condition. Surfaced prominently across all provider views.
📤 Referrals & Documents
FHIR: ServiceRequest, DocumentReference. Structured referrals, attached letters and imaging.

7. Break-Glass — Emergency Access

A protocol, not a button. The provider sees friction. The patient sees a notification. Compliance sees every event.

⚡ Break-glass access. Patient consent is not on file. Invoking emergency access will be reviewed by compliance. The patient will be notified immediately.

Justification required

Life-threatening emergency — patient unconscious
